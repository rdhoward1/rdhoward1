### Howdy! I’m Rachel! 🧬
---
I am a computational biologist who uses large 'omics data to study disease in a holobiont system. My work brings together host–microbe biology, bioinformatics, and machine learning to understand how organisms and their microbial partners respond to disease. I integrate 'omics data blocks to identify candidate biomarkers and investigate the biological processes behind those patterns.

In my latest project, I developed predictive machine learning models that integrated host and symbiont gene expression with microbiome profiles from a holobiont system to identify and prioritize candidate biomarkers that distinguished healthy from disease-treated samples.

I am currently working on building a pan-transcriptome for my study system and expanding my skills in single-cell and spatial transcriptomics!

I’m a computational biologist studying how corals, their algal symbionts, and their microbiomes respond to disease.
