### 🌊 Hello there! I'm Rachel! 
---
I am a computational biologist who uses large 'omics data to study disease in a holobiont system. My work brings together host–microbe biology, bioinformatics, and machine learning to understand how organisms and their microbial partners respond to disease. I integrate 'omics data blocks to identify candidate biomarkers and investigate the biological processes behind those patterns.

In my most recent project, I used machine learning to perform N-integration on gene expression and microbiome data from my holobiont system to identify robust markers of disease. 

I am currently working on building a pan-transcriptome for my study system, while learning spatial transcriptomics and single cell-analyses on the side. 

#### Here's a little background on me: 

* 🎓 Ph.D., Soil, Water, and Ecosystem Sciences (University of Florida)
* 🎓 B.S., Marine Biology (Roger Williams University) 
* 👩‍💻 Postdoc at University of Texas at Arlington
* 🧫 Prepping for a transition to industry
* 📍 DFW, TX
